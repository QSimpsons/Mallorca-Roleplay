snelle-fatbike — alles-in-één
=============================

1. Zet DEZE map (snelle-fatbike) direct in resources.
   Juiste pad: resources/snelle-fatbike/fxmanifest.lua
   (niet: resources/snelle-fatbike/snelle-fatbike/...)

2. server.cfg — alleen dit:

   ensure snelle-fatbike

3. Herstart de server.
4. In-game: /fatbike (toets G)

Streamfiles staan in stream/ (snellefat.yft/.ytd).
